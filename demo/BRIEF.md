# Demo brief — AURI A1 (fictional brand for team testing)

**Product**: AURI A1 — premium wireless over-ear headphones, direct-to-consumer.
**Price**: $349 (one-time purchase, 30-day returns, 2-year warranty, free shipping).
**Audience**: professionals 28–45 doing deep-focus work (remote/hybrid), value silence and sound quality, already own cheaper headphones and were let down by them.
**Competitive alternative**: flagship ANC models ($399–449) and the "good enough" $150 tier.
**Differentiators**: adaptive ANC that learns what distracts the wearer; 60h battery (double the class); studio-tuned titanium drivers; repairability (swappable parts, 10 years of support).
**Buyer awareness stage**: solution-aware (they know they want ANC headphones; they don't know AURI).
**Page goal**: one conversion — purchase (add to cart). Secondary: email capture for the not-ready.

**Team assignment**: one sales page (`demo/index.html` + `demo/tokens.css` + `demo/styles.css`),
static (no framework), mobile-first, built strictly to the `skills/` rules.
QA gates: `spacing-lint` and `contrast-check` clean, a11y checklist, Art Director with zero BLOCKER/MAJOR findings.
